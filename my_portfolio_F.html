About My Research Focus

I am Raksha Sri A, an AI Researcher specializing in machine learning algorithms for biomedical signal processing, complex decision systems, and high-speed computer vision.

Currently pursuing a Direct PhD, my work centers on bridging fundamental deep learning research with scalable real-world deployments. I focus on developing robust models capable of processing temporal high-dimensional data (such as multi-channel EEG signals) and autonomous optimization via deep reinforcement learning.
